# Math for AI

The four pieces of mathematics that deep learning actually runs on — explained
from intuition first, formulas second, and each mapped to the chapter of
*Mathematics for Machine Learning* (Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong — free PDF from the
authors) where the full story lives.

| Page | The question it answers | MML book |
|---|---|---|
| [Linear algebra & matmul](linear-algebra/) | why is everything a matrix multiplication? | ch. 2–3 |
| [Calculus & gradients](calculus-gradients/) | how does a network know which way to adjust? | ch. 5 |
| [Probability, logits & softmax](probability-softmax/) | how do raw scores become probabilities? | ch. 6 |
| [Optimization: SGD to AdamW](optimization/) | how do millions of weights actually move? | ch. 7 |

**How to use this section:** read a page here first (15 minutes, intuition and
the formulas that matter), then go deep in the corresponding MML chapter when
you want proofs and rigor. The [glossary](../glossary/) holds the two-sentence
versions of every term.
